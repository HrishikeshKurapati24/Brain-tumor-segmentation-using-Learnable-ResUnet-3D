Brain Tumor Segmentation – Aggregated Experimental Results
============================================================

1) Variant Comparison: LearnableFusionResUNet3D vs ResUNet3D
------------------------------------------------------------

1.1) High-level tumor metrics and training budget
-------------------------------------------------
Two models are compared:
- LearnableFusionResUNet3D: includes learnable MRI fusion, dropout-based regularization, and 20 training epochs.
- Baseline ResUNet3D: no dropout, no learnable fusion, trained for 10 epochs.
- Both models were trained under the same compute constraints (2× NVIDIA T4 GPUs on Kaggle).
- The goal: evaluate how much multimodal fusion and dropout stabilize and improve segmentation.

| Metric                          | LearnableFusionResUNet3D | ResUNet3D (baseline) |
|---------------------------------|---------------------------|-----------------------|
| Macro Tumor Dice (1–4)          | 0.8292                    | 0.6851                |
| Whole Tumor Dice (WT)           | 0.8563                    | 0.7811                |
| Tumor Core Dice (TC)            | 0.9056                    | 0.8504                |
| Enhancing Tumor Dice (ET)       | 0.8430                    | 0.7418                |
| Best validation metric           | 0.7515                    | 0.7097                |
| Best epoch                      | 13                        | 3                     |
| Total epochs                    | 20                        | 10 (early stopping)   |

- LearnableFusionResUNet3D achieves substantially higher Dice across all major metrics.
- Macro Tumor Dice increases from 0.685 → 0.829.
- WT/TC/ET improve significantly, especially Enhancing Tumor.
- Best epoch occurs later, showing stable learning with dropout and fusion.
- Longer training schedule allows deeper feature refinement.

Summary of Variant Comparison
------------------------------
- Learnable fusion + dropout improves macro tumor Dice from ~0.685 → ~0.829.
- WT, TC, ET metrics all improve.
- Per-class Dice and IoU show consistent gains.
- LearnableFusionResUNet3D is substantially superior to the baseline.

4) Compute Constraints and Discussion
-------------------------------------
- Training done on Kaggle using 2× T4 GPUs.
- No extensive hyperparameter tuning.
- Short training schedules due to time limits.
- Despite constraints, model achieves strong metrics.

4.1) On-par results under limited compute
-----------------------------------------
- Achieves ~83% macro Dice, strong WT/TC/ET scores.
- Comparable to Res-UNet (86–90.6% MFCL).
- Much lower compute than Swin-UNet-EPA.

4.2) Effect of architecture choices
-----------------------------------
- Learnable fusion + dropout gives largest performance improvement.
- Improvements occur across all Dice/IoU metrics.
- Shows architecture is effective even under compute limits.

5) Fusion Strategy Ablation (Learnable Fusion)
----------------------------------------------
See figure: ResUnet3D/fusion_ablation.png

Learnable fusion shows the strongest improvement trend:
- Learns importance of each modality adaptively.
- Expected to surpass static fusion in long training.
- Most scalable and generalizable fusion strategy.

6) Loss Function Ablation (Dice + Focal is Superior)
----------------------------------------------------
See figure: ResUnet3D/loss_ablation.png

Dice + Focal:
- Achieves highest macro Dice (~0.81).
- Balances region overlap + prevents class collapse.
- Outperforms Dice-only, Focal-only, and Cross Entropy.
